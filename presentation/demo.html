Healthcare Hackathon Bayern · IFOHRA Challenge 9

Smart RoboWard

A hacked delivery robot should never stop a hospital.

The problem

One lift request, three open questions

Who is asking?

Orders arrive over the network. Anyone can send one.

Is this floor allowed?

A genuine robot can still request the wrong floor.

Who holds the parcel?

When the trip stops, the ward needs to know.

What we built

Two gates and a fallback

Request gate

Signed single-use orders, ward rules, minimal data, a circuit breaker.

Lift gate

Allow, hold or deny each call for this trip.

Human fallback

A porter takes over. The ward scans exactly once.
