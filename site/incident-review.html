How one incident runs in the live simulation

  1. 1 · DetectedThe gate counts refusals per sender. Five bad requests, or more than five in a second, trip the breaker.
  2. 2 · Alert sentAn alert is written to the audit chain. It names the Security duty lead and carries the evidence.
  3. 3 · Robots isolatedAll robots stay cut off. Nothing resets by itself, and nurse tasks go by hand.
  4. 4 · Supervisor restoresOnly a supervisor can restore the robots. Then the next task goes by robot again.

The vendor-lift case (a vendor account asking for the lift with no delivery) and this alert and restore flow are checked by tests and shown in the live simulation. They are not part of the 20-request benchmark above.
